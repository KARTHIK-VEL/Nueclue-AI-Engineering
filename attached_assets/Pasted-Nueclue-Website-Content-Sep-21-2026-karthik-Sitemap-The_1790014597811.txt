3. GPU Flex (Product Page)
Hero
Headline: GPU Flex: every GPU, fully used.
Subheadline: GPU Flex turns NVIDIA GPU servers into a shared, managed compute platform. Users get instant browser-based access, administrators get full control and live visibility, and your hardware gets used the way it should be.
Buttons: [Request a Demo] [Talk to Our Team]
The Problem
GPU servers are among the largest technology investments an organisation makes, yet utilisation is often low:
• One user or team occupies an entire GPU for a small workload
• New users wait days for environment setup and access
• No scheduling or allocation rules, so GPUs are either idle or contested
• Administrators have no real-time view of who is using what
• No usage data to justify spend or plan capacity
GPU Flex solves these at the platform level.
What GPU Flex Does
Flexible GPU allocation
• Shared GPUs for notebooks, coursework, and light experimentation
• Dedicated GPUs for training runs and research that need full performance
• MIG-partitioned GPUs that split one physical GPU into multiple isolated instances, each with its own memory and compute
The mix is configurable and can change as demand changes.
Multiple access methods
• JupyterHub: notebooks in the browser, with no installation
• Web Terminal: a full Linux terminal from any browser
• SSH / SFTP: direct access for advanced users and file transfer
• Admin Dashboard: central control for administrators
Containerised environments: each user works in an isolated container with preconfigured PyTorch, TensorFlow, Hugging Face, and CUDA libraries.
Shared storage: centralised network storage for datasets, models, and project files.
Live monitoring dashboard
• Real-time utilisation, memory, and temperature for every GPU
• Active users, containers, and sessions at a glance
• Allocation view showing shared, dedicated, and MIG assignments
• Usage trends and history for reporting and capacity planning
Smart utilisation alerts: GPU Flex flags contention based on actual load, such as high compute utilisation or VRAM pressure, rather than simply counting users.
User and access management: create users, assign GPUs or MIG instances, set access levels, and manage sessions from one place.
Who GPU Flex Is For
Enterprises & MNCs: consolidate GPU servers into one managed pool shared across teams, track usage by team, and get more from existing hardware before buying more.
Startups: run training, fine-tuning, and inference on your own GPUs with the convenience of a cloud platform, without cloud pricing.
Universities & Research Institutions: reliable browser-based GPU access for hundreds of students and researchers. [See GPU Labs for Institutions →]
Built on NVIDIA Technology
GPU Flex runs on NVIDIA data centre GPUs, including DGX-class systems, using NVIDIA drivers and CUDA, Multi-Instance GPU (MIG), NGC containers and Docker, with multi-node support for scaled deployments.
Deployment & Services
GPU Flex is delivered as a managed deployment on your hardware, on-premise, with your data staying inside your network.
1. Assessment: we review your hardware, users, workloads, and current utilisation.
2. Allocation design: we plan the shared, dedicated, and MIG configuration.
3. Deployment: we set up the platform, access methods, storage, and dashboard.
4. Onboarding: we train administrators and onboard users.
5. Managed support: monitoring, maintenance, updates, and allocation tuning.
Already have GPUs that are underused? We start with a utilisation audit of your existing setup and show where capacity is being lost, before recommending any changes.
Why GPU Flex
• Higher utilisation: partitioning and flexible allocation keep GPUs working
• Faster onboarding: users start in minutes through the browser
• Full visibility: live dashboards replace guesswork
• Data stays on-premise: no data leaves your infrastructure
• Operated by engineers who run it: we administer production GPU Flex environments ourselves
FAQ
Does GPU Flex require specific hardware? It is designed for NVIDIA data centre GPUs. MIG requires supported GPUs such as A100, H100, H200, and B200.
Can we change allocations later? Yes. Shared, dedicated, and MIG configurations can be adjusted as usage changes.
Is it a cloud service? No. GPU Flex runs on your own hardware.
How many users can share one server? It depends on the GPUs and workloads. We size this during assessment.

Why Enterprise AI Projects Stall
Most organisations have run an AI pilot. Far fewer have one running in production. The usual blockers:
• Proofs of concept built on sample data that fail on real data
• No evaluation framework, so accuracy can't be measured or trusted
• Difficulty integrating with ERP, CRM, and legacy systems
• Data-privacy concerns about sending sensitive data to external APIs
• No plan for monitoring, maintenance, or model updates after launch
We build with these constraints in mind from the first sprint.
What We Build
LLM applications and internal copilots: assistants that answer questions from your policies, manuals, contracts, and internal knowledge, grounded in your documents using RAG so answers trace back to sources.
Document intelligence: extraction, classification, and summarisation of invoices, purchase orders, contracts, forms, and reports, including scanned documents.
Conversational analytics: natural-language access to business data. Managers ask questions by chat or voice and get answers with charts.
Computer vision systems: defect detection, safety and PPE monitoring, people counting, access control, and visual inspection, on cloud or edge devices.
Speech and voice systems: transcription, call analytics, voice interfaces, and multilingual speech processing, including Indian languages.
Predictive models: demand forecasting, predictive maintenance, churn prediction, and risk scoring.
Our Approach
1. Use-case assessment: where AI will deliver measurable value, and where it won't.
2. Data and architecture review: data sources, quality, access, and deployment model.
3. Model selection: open-source models (Llama, Mistral, Qwen) and commercial APIs evaluated against accuracy, latency, cost, and privacy.
4. Build and evaluate: two-week sprints with test datasets and accuracy benchmarks.
5. Integrate and deploy: API integration with your systems on cloud, on-premise, or hybrid.
6. Monitor and improve: performance tracking, model updates, and maintenance.
Deployment Options
• Cloud: fastest to launch, scales on demand
• On-premise: data never leaves your infrastructure, suited to healthcare, finance, and regulated sectors
• Hybrid: sensitive processing on-premise, other workloads in the cloud
• Edge: models on devices at factories, gates, or remote sites for low-latency processing
Technology Stack
• Models: commercial LLM APIs, Llama, Mistral, Qwen, Whisper, YOLO, and custom-trained models
• Frameworks: PyTorch, Hugging Face, LangChain, LlamaIndex
• Data: vector databases, SQL and NoSQL databases, data pipelines
• Serving: vLLM, TensorRT, Triton Inference Server, FastAPI
• Infrastructure: NVIDIA GPUs, Docker, Kubernetes, major cloud platforms
Security and Governance
• Role-based access control on all AI applications
• Audit logs for queries and outputs
• Guardrails that restrict responses to approved topics and data
• Private deployments with no data sent to third-party model providers, where required
• Data handling aligned with India's DPDP Act

Do we need a lot of data to start? Not always. Many LLM and RAG applications work with existing documents.
Who owns the code and models? You do. We hand over source code, documentation, and trained model weights.
Can you work with our in-house IT team? Yes, including knowledge transfer so they can maintain the system.

5. AI Agents & Automation
Hero
Headline: AI Agents & Automation
Subheadline: Agents that connect to your business systems and complete tasks: answering calls, booking appointments, qualifying leads, and resolving support queries, around the clock.
Buttons: [Discuss Your Use Case] [See a Case Study]
Agents vs Chatbots
A chatbot answers questions. An agent takes action. AI agents understand requests in natural language, decide what needs to be done, and act on your systems: checking availability, creating bookings, updating CRM records, sending confirmations, and escalating to a person when needed.
Agents We Build
Voice Agents
• Answer inbound calls and make outbound reminder calls
• Book, reschedule, and cancel appointments in real time
• Handle common questions on pricing, timings, and location
• Speak English, Tamil, Hindi, and other Indian languages
• Transfer to staff with full call context when needed
Common in hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centres, salons, service centres, and venues.
Sales & Lead Agents
• Respond to inbound leads within seconds on web, WhatsApp, or phone
• Qualify leads with structured questions
• Run automated follow-up sequences
• Book meetings into your sales team's calendar
• Log every interaction in your CRM
Customer Support Agents
• Answer questions from your documentation and past tickets
• Track orders, handle returns, and update customer records
• Escalate complex cases to human agents with full context
• Available on web chat, WhatsApp, email, and phone
Operations Agents
• Generate and distribute daily or weekly reports
• Reconcile data across systems
• Process routine requests such as leave, approvals, and data entry
• Monitor metrics and send alerts
How Our Agents Work
1. Understand: speech recognition and LLMs interpret the request, including accents, regional languages, and mixed-language conversations.
2. Decide: the agent determines the right action based on your business rules.
3. Act: it calls your systems through APIs: calendars, CRMs, booking platforms, databases.
4. Confirm: it confirms the outcome by voice, SMS, WhatsApp, or email.
5. Hand off: it transfers to a person when the request falls outside defined boundaries.
Built for Reliability
• Guardrails: agents act only within the tasks and information you approve
• Human handoff: clear escalation rules for complex or sensitive cases
• Conversation logs: every interaction recorded and searchable
• Performance dashboards: resolution rate, handoff rate, call volume, and response times
• Continuous improvement: regular review of failed conversations
Integrations
Google Calendar and Outlook, CRMs, WhatsApp Business, telephony and IVR systems, hospital and clinic management software, e-commerce platforms, and custom databases via API.
Implementation Process
1. Workflow mapping: the conversations and actions the agent needs to handle.
2. Integration setup: calendar, CRM, telephony, and other systems.
3. Build and test: tested against real conversation scenarios.
4. Pilot launch: limited volume, with every interaction monitored.
5. Full rollout: scale-up with ongoing monitoring and tuning.
FAQ
Will the agent sound robotic? We use modern speech synthesis that sounds natural, with conversation flows designed around how your customers speak.
Can it handle regional languages? Yes, including mixed-language conversations.
What if the agent doesn't understand? It asks a clarifying question, and if still unsure, transfers to staff with the conversation context.
How long does setup take? A single-workflow agent such as appointment booking typically launches in 3 to 6 weeks.

Page Header
Headline: About Nueclue
Intro: Nueclue is an AI engineering firm based in Bengaluru, India. We build AI applications and the GPU infrastructure behind them for enterprises, startups, and academic institutions.
Our Focus
We started Nueclue to close a gap we saw repeatedly. Organisations invest in AI models, tools, and expensive GPU hardware, but struggle to turn that investment into systems that run reliably in production.
Our work sits across the full stack: the application layer, where models meet business workflows, and the infrastructure layer, where compute is provisioned, shared, and optimised through our GPU Flex platform. Handling both lets us make practical decisions on model size, deployment target, and hardware utilisation that single-layer vendors often miss.
What Sets Our Work Apart
• Production-first engineering: evaluation metrics, monitoring, access control, and documentation are part of every build
• Application and infrastructure under one team: the engineers who build your AI system understand the GPUs it runs on
• Practical model choices: open-source models on your hardware or commercial APIs, based on data sensitivity, latency, accuracy, and budget
• Code and knowledge handover: clients receive source code, documentation, and walkthroughs. You own what we build.

13. FAQ
Page Header
Headline: Frequently Asked Questions
Intro: Answers to the questions we hear most often about working with Nueclue. If yours isn't here, contact us.
Working With Us
What kind of clients do you work with? Enterprises and MNCs, startups at idea or growth stage, and colleges, universities, and research institutions.
Where are you based? Do you work with clients outside Bengaluru? We're based in Bengaluru and work remotely with clients across India and internationally. On-site visits are arranged for infrastructure deployments and training.
How does a project start? With a free discovery call, followed by a written proposal covering scope, technical approach, timeline, and cost.
Do you sign NDAs? Yes, before any detailed discussion of your idea, data, or systems.
Pricing and Engagement
How do you price projects? Three models: fixed-scope pricing for well-defined projects, monthly retainers for ongoing development and support, and short paid discovery sprints when requirements need validation first.
Can you give a quote before understanding our requirement? We can give a rough range after the discovery call. A firm quote comes with the written proposal.
Do you offer ongoing support after delivery? Yes, through maintenance retainers covering bug fixes, model updates, monitoring, and infrastructure tuning.
Code, Data, and Security
Who owns the code and IP? You do. On completion and payment, we hand over source code, documentation, and trained model weights.
Will our data be used to train other models? No. Your data is used only for your project.
Can AI systems run without sending data to external AI providers? Yes. We deploy open-source models on your own servers or private cloud, so data never leaves your environment.
How do you handle data protection? We follow access controls, least-privilege access, and handling practices aligned with India's DPDP Act.
AI Development and Agents
How long does an AI project take? A focused use case usually reaches production in 6 to 12 weeks. A single-workflow AI agent typically launches in 3 to 6 weeks. Startup MVPs usually take 6 to 12 weeks after discovery.
Do we need a lot of data to start? Not always. Many LLM and RAG applications work with existing documents. We assess data readiness during scoping.
Do your voice agents support Indian languages? Yes: English, Tamil, Hindi, and other Indian languages, including mixed-language conversations.
GPU Flex and Infrastructure
What is GPU Flex? Our platform for NVIDIA GPU servers. It provides shared, dedicated, and MIG-partitioned allocation, browser access through JupyterHub and web terminal, and a live dashboard for utilisation and users.
Does GPU Flex run in the cloud? No. It runs on your own hardware, on-premise.
We already own GPUs. Can you help? Yes. We start with a utilisation audit and improve access, scheduling, and monitoring on your existing hardware.
Do you supply GPU hardware? We advise on configuration and sizing, and can coordinate procurement through authorised channels.
Do you provide training for our team or students? Yes: administrator training, faculty development programs, and hands-on student workshops.
